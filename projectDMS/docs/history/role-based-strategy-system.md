# Role-Based Strategic Planning System with Consolidated Three-Way Context

## Overview

This document provides a comprehensive role-based strategic planning system for infrastructure projects with three-way correspondence between **Contractor**, **Engineer's Representative**, and **Employer**. The system generates consolidated contexts from complete letter content and provides role-specific prompts for developing strategic plans.

## 2. Three-Way Consolidated Contexts

### 2.1 Contractor Context (AFCONS-SAM India Consortium)

**Position Summary:**
The Contractor claims reimbursement for the third borewell construction (Rs. 49,57,493/-) based on the Employer's direct instruction via NOTE-104 and the Engineer's subsequent letter directing them to proceed with the work.

**Complete Chronological Context:**

**Letter 1: AFC/PM/KNPCC-06/1790 (29-Jul-2023)**
- **Content**: Contractor originally proposed THREE borewells to facilitate water connections for the local community whose borewells fell on the tunnel alignment from Nayaganj to Kanpur Central:
  1. Infront of Laxmi Narayan Temple near Dhana Khori (300mm dia.)
  2. Lucknow Fatak, Dudhmandi footpath (300mm dia.)
  3. Near Gagan Plaza (300mm dia.)
- Permissions from utility departments already obtained
- Subsequently received input from MLA and local representatives to revise locations to: Danakhori (Sidharth Hotel) & Gagan Plaza
- Requested Engineer's support in communicating with Jal Kal Vibhag & Nagar Nigam

**Letter 2: AFC/PM/KNPCC-06/3975 (06-Nov-2024)**
- **Content**: Contractor acknowledges Engineer's letter (ref. Kanpur-LET-JVTI-TBM-00371-E01 dated 06.09.2024) referring to Employer's NOTE-104
- States they are commencing the 3rd borewell drive near Gagan Plaza as per growing demand from local community
- **Key Claim**: "whose cost shall be reimbursed by the Employer"
- No contractual analysis provided at this stage

**Letter 3: AFC/PM/KNPCC-06/4096 (12-Dec-2024)**
- **Content**: Contractor reports that due to space constraints, boring machine cannot reach Gagan Plaza location
- Alternate location identified: Harbansh Mohal near Kuda Ghar, Ravidas Mandir
- Joint visit conducted with Jal Kal Executive Engineer, MLA, Parshad, UPMRC-XEN, AFCONS representative
- Requests Engineer to obtain approval/NOC from Jal Kal Vibhag & Nagar Nigam for alternate location
- References existing NOCs (Letter 110 dated 23.06.2023 and Letter 261 dated 16.08.2023)

**Letter 4: AFC/PM/KNPCC-06/4399 (24-Mar-2025)**
- **Content**: Borewell installation at Kuda Ghar, Ravidas Mandir, Harbansh Mohal successfully completed
- Requests handover to Jal Kal Vibhag - Nagar Nigam, Kanpur
- Submits water quality test report
- No cost claim raised at this stage

**Letter 5: AFC/PM/KNPCC-06/4670 (03-Jul-2025)**
- **Content**: Formal submission of construction cost: **Rs. 49,57,493/-**
- **Key Arguments**:
  1. Joint survey conducted earlier, two borewells of 300mm diameter already constructed for ~160 houses
  2. Engineer's instruction letter (06.09.2024) explicitly directed Contractor to construct third borewell
  3. Third borewell connected to Jal Kal Vibhag main water supply tanks (not directly to local residents)
  4. Employer's letter (Kanpur-LET-JVTI-TBM-00395-E01) references Employer's Requirements Section-A, General Part-1, Clause 12: Survey and Site Investigations
  5. Handing Over Note (30.03.2024) confirms borewells were constructed per authority standards
  6. NOC from Jal Kal Vibhag submitted via letter 3080 dated 06.04.2024
- Reserves rights to amend submission

**Letter 6: AFC/PM/KNPCC-06/4905 (05-Nov-2025)**
- **Content**: Reminder letter for determination of Rs. 49,57,493/-
- **Key Arguments**:
  1. Cost details already submitted via letter 4670 dated 03.07.2025 - determination still awaited
  2. Two borewells already constructed to utility owner's satisfaction with NOC issued
  3. **Critical Point**: Third borewell constructed **"only on account of instructions issued by UPMRC/GC via Note No. UPMRC/CPM-1/KNPCC-06/2024-25/Vol-2/117/104 dated 05-09-2024"**
  4. Therefore, cost for additional works must be reimbursed
- Reserves rights to amend submission
- Offers availability for consultation to facilitate determination

**Contractor's Position:**
- Originally planned THREE borewells as part of initial proposal (July 2023)
- Only TWO borewells were executed initially (Doodhmandi and Sidharth Hotel)
- The THIRD borewell was explicitly instructed by Employer (NOTE-104) and Engineer (Letter 371-E01)
- Location changed due to technical constraints (space for boring machine)
- Work completed in good faith following direct instructions
- Cost incurred: Rs. 49,57,493/- and should be reimbursed as variation/additional work

### 2.2 Engineer's Representative Context (TYPSA-ITALFERR JV)

**Position Summary:**
The Engineer rejects the Contractor's claim, stating that the third borewell was always part of the Contractor's original proposal (July 2023) and is covered under the lump sum contract price. No additional payment is admissible.

**Complete Chronological Context:**

**Letter 1: Kanpur-LET-JVTI-TBM-00371-E01 (06-Sep-2024)**
- **Content**: Engineer forwards Employer's NOTE-104 to Contractor
- States: "We are in receipt of a 'NOTE' cited above from the Employer regarding the installation of a borewell near Gagan Plaza"
- **Instruction**: "The Contractor is requested to proceed with the work of installation of the borewell and submit a compliance report after successful completion"
- Neutral tone - simply forwarding Employer's instruction
- No mention of payment or contractual implications
- Enclosure: UPMRC NOTE-104

**Letter 2: Kanpur-LET-JVTI-TBM-00395-E01 (07-Nov-2024)**
- **Content**: Engineer receives Contractor's letter AFC/PM/KNPCC-06/3975 claiming reimbursement
- **Rejection Position**: "The Contractor has stated that the cost of the 3rd Borewell drive shall be reimbursed by the Employer which is not admissible as detailed below"
- **Key Arguments**:
  1. **Reference to Original Proposal**: "GC would like to draw the Contractor's attention to its proposal made vide submission stated at ref. (3) above [AFC/PM/KNPCC-06/1790 dated 29.07.2023]"
  2. "The proposal clearly shows the provision of **three (3) borewells** for facilitating the water line connection to the local community whose borewells were falling on the tunnel alignment from Nayaganj to Kanpur Central"
  3. **Current Status**: "As of now, only two borewells have been executed by the Contractor, one at Doodhmandi and another near Siddhartha Hotel"
  4. **Public Demand Evidence**: "And there has been a clear public outcry reflected in recent communications concerning the insufficient water supply, underscoring the urgent need for third borewell"
  5. **Contractual Reference**: Cites **UPMRC/KNPCC-06/Volume-3/ Employer's Requirements/ Section–A/General Part-1, 12. SURVEY AND SITE INVESTIGATIONS. (3)**:
     - "The Contractor shall carry out survey of existing Bore holes/Tube wells etc. falls in the Zone of Inference in the Tunnel Alignment and submit a report to the Engineer"
     - "The Contractor shall relocate/shift these identified Boreholes/Tube wells at alternate location with the consent & satisfaction of the owner of these bore holes/ Tube wells with prior approval of Engineer"
     - **Critical Clause**: "**Nothing extra shall be paid to the contractor for relocation shifting of these Bore holes/ Tube wells as this will be inclusive in fixed lump sum price under Schedule 'A'**"
  6. **Conclusion**: "Thus, the third borewell which is now being executed near Gagan Plaza, is part of the Contractor's proposal and hence **no separate payment shall be made for this work**"
  7. "The Contractor shall complete the third borewell including laying of necessary pipelines, testing & commissioning, and handover to the owning agency **as per its Contractual Obligations at no additional cost to the Employer**"

**Engineer's Position:**
- Contractor's original July 2023 submission clearly proposed THREE borewells
- Only TWO were executed initially, leaving the third pending
- The third borewell fulfills the Contractor's original proposal and contractual obligations
- Clause 12 of Employer's Requirements explicitly states relocation of borewells is included in lump sum price
- No variation order was issued for this work
- Therefore, no additional payment is admissible

### 3.2 Prompt Template: Engineer Writing to Contractor

```
ROLE: Engineer's Representative

You are the Engineer's Representative responsible for reviewing and replying to contractor correspondence on a major infrastructure project governed by FIDIC or similar contract conditions. The Contractor has submitted letter [{LETTER_REFERENCE}] dated [{LETTER_DATE}], for which a formal contractual reply is to be issued.

All prior correspondence (both from the Contractor and the Engineer/Employer) on this topic or related subject is available in the consolidated contexts below.

Your task is to develop a comprehensive strategic plan and outline for the Engineer's reply, based on a thorough review of all relevant letters.

---

=== CONSOLIDATED CONTRACTOR CONTEXT ===
{CONTRACTOR_FULL_CONTEXT}

=== CONSOLIDATED ENGINEER CONTEXT ===
{ENGINEER_FULL_CONTEXT}

=== CONSOLIDATED EMPLOYER CONTEXT ===
{EMPLOYER_FULL_CONTEXT}

---

Your strategic plan must include the following sections:

## 1. ISSUE SUMMARY
Provide a brief summary (3-5 sentences) of the Contractor's current request, claim, or submission. State the core issue clearly without interpretation at this stage.

## 2. CHRONOLOGY
Create a detailed timeline of all relevant communications, including:
- Date, reference number, sender, and subject for each letter
- Key developments or shifts in position
- Any procedural milestones (e.g., NOCs obtained, site visits, handovers)

## 3. CONTRACTOR'S POSITION
Extract and summarize:
- **Key Claims**: What is the Contractor claiming or requesting?
- **Arguments Presented**: What reasons or justifications has the Contractor provided?
- **Evidence Cited**: What documents, clauses, or prior communications does the Contractor reference?
- **Assumptions Made**: What implicit or explicit assumptions underlie the Contractor's position?
- **Cost Implications** (if applicable): Specific amounts claimed and basis

## 4. ENGINEER/EMPLOYER'S PREVIOUS STANDPOINT
Summarize:
- Any prior responses or positions taken by the Engineer or Employer
- Instructions or directives issued
- Contractual interpretations previously communicated
- Consistency or inconsistency with current Contractor submission

## 5. CONTRACTUAL REFERENCE
Identify and analyze all relevant contractual provisions:
- **General Conditions of Contract (GCC)**: Relevant clauses (e.g., Clause 8, 13, 20)
- **Special Conditions of Contract (SCC)**: Any modifications to GCC
- **Employer's Requirements (ER)**: Specific technical or procedural requirements
- **Bill of Quantities (BOQ)**: Payment items and lump sum provisions
- **Schedule of Rates**: Applicable rates if claiming variation

For each clause:
- Quote the exact text
- Analyze whether it supports or contradicts the Contractor's claim
- Note any ambiguities or gaps

## 6. EVALUATION

### 6.1 Validity Assessment
Assess the Contractor's claim on:
- **Contractual Basis**: Is the claim grounded in specific contract provisions?
- **Procedural Compliance**: Has the Contractor followed required notice periods, submission formats, or approval processes (e.g., Clause 20 requirements)?
- **Factual Accuracy**: Are the facts stated by the Contractor accurate per available records?
- **Precedent Considerations**: Does this claim set or follow any prior precedent on the project?

### 6.2 Variation vs. Original Obligation
Determine:
- Does the work constitute a **variation** (change in scope, additional work, or changed conditions)?
- OR is it part of the Contractor's **original contractual obligations** (included in lump sum or existing scope)?
- What is the basis for this determination?

### 6.3 Procedural Compliance Check
Verify:
- **Notice Requirements**: Was timely notice given per contract (e.g., Clause 20.1)?
- **Approval Requirements**: Was Engineer's approval obtained before commencing work (if required)?
- **Submission Format**: Are all required documents, cost breakdowns, or justifications provided?

## 7. RECOMMENDED REPLY STRATEGY

### 7.1 Tone and Framing
- **Overall Tone**: [Firm / Cooperative / Neutral / Cautious]
- **Relationship Considerations**: How to maintain professional working relationship while protecting Employer's interests
- **Precedent Awareness**: Implications of acceptance/rejection for future similar claims

### 7.2 Key Messages to Include
Specify the core messages the reply must convey:
- Acceptance (full or partial)
- Rejection with clear reasons
- Request for clarification or additional information
- Conditional approval pending further submissions
- Interpretation of contractual provisions
- Instructions for next steps

### 7.3 Required Documents or Actions
List what must be requested from the Contractor (if applicable):
- Detailed cost breakdown with supporting invoices
- Compliance reports or completion certificates
- Revised submissions addressing specific deficiencies
- Clarifications on specific technical or procedural points

### 7.4 Draft Structure
Outline the structure of the reply letter:
1. **Opening**: Acknowledge receipt and reference relevant correspondence
2. **Background**: Summarize the issue and chronology (brief)
3. **Engineer's Position**: State the contractual basis and determination
4. **Justification**: Provide detailed reasoning with clause references
5. **Instruction/Decision**: Clear directive or decision
6. **Next Steps**: What the Contractor must do (if applicable)
7. **Closing**: Professional closing with contact for clarifications

## 8. RISK/IMPACT NOTES

### 8.1 Commercial Risks
- Financial exposure to Employer if claim is accepted
- Precedent for similar claims across project
- Impact on project budget and contingencies

### 8.2 Technical Risks
- Implications of accepting/rejecting on project execution
- Dependencies on this work for other activities
- Quality or safety considerations

### 8.3 Relationship Risks
- Potential for dispute or escalation if claim rejected
- Impact on Contractor cooperation and project schedule
- Reputational considerations with Employer or third parties

### 8.4 Legal Risks
- Risk of arbitration or litigation if determination is disputed
- Strength of contractual position if challenged
- Potential for counterclaims or setoffs

## 9. CONCLUSION & FINAL RECOMMENDATION

Provide a clear, concise recommendation:
- **Recommended Course of Action**: [Accept / Reject / Request More Information / Conditional Approval]
- **Rationale** (2-3 sentences): Why this is the most appropriate response
- **Alternative Options** (if applicable): Other possible approaches and their trade-offs
- **Next Steps for Engineer**: Internal actions required (e.g., consult Employer, review contract, engage legal)

---

**IMPORTANT CONSTRAINTS:**
- Base your analysis ONLY on the information provided in the consolidated contexts
- Do NOT invent clause numbers, dates, or facts not explicitly mentioned
- If critical information is missing, explicitly state: "Information Required: [specific detail needed]"
- Maintain objectivity and professional tone throughout
- Cite specific letter references when referring to positions or claims
```

async def generate_consolidated_context(
    letter_id: str,
    role: str,  # "contractor", "engineer", "employer"
    current_user: dict = Depends(verify_auth)
):
    """
    Generate consolidated context from all linked letters in thread.
    Uses complete letter content instead of summaries.
    """
    
    # Get all letters in thread
    thread_letters = await LetterService.get_thread_letters(letter_id)
    
    # Consolidate by role
    context = await ContextService.consolidate_context_by_role(
        thread_letters=thread_letters,
        role=role
    )
    
    return {
        "role": role,
        "context": context,
        "letter_count": len(thread_letters),
        "parties": ["Contractor", "Engineer", "Employer"]
    }

@router.post("/api/ai-assistant/langgraph/strategy-plan")
async def generate_strategy_plan(
    request: StrategyPlanRequest,
    current_user: dict = Depends(verify_auth)
):
    """Generate role-based strategic plan"""
    
    # Get consolidated contexts
    contractor_context = await ContextService.get_contractor_context(request.letter_id)
    engineer_context = await ContextService.get_engineer_context(request.letter_id)
    employer_context = await ContextService.get_employer_context(request.letter_id)
    
    # Select prompt based on role
    if request.role == "contractor":
        prompt_template = CONTRACTOR_TO_ENGINEER_PROMPT
    elif request.role == "engineer":
        if request.recipient == "Contractor":
            prompt_template = ENGINEER_TO_CONTRACTOR_PROMPT
        else:
            prompt_template = ENGINEER_TO_EMPLOYER_PROMPT
    
    # Inject contexts into prompt
    full_prompt = prompt_template.format(
        CONTRACTOR_FULL_CONTEXT=contractor_context,
        ENGINEER_FULL_CONTEXT=engineer_context,
        EMPLOYER_FULL_CONTEXT=employer_context,
        LETTER_REFERENCE=request.letter_reference,
        LETTER_DATE=request.letter_date
    )
    
    # Run LangGraph with role-specific prompt
    result = await AIService.generate_strategy_plan(
        prompt=full_prompt,
        role=request.role
    )
    
    return result
