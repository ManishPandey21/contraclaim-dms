# Claim Procedure Quick Reference

## FIDIC 1999 / 2017
- Notice of Claim: within 28 days of awareness (Sub-Clause 20.1 / 20.2)
- Fully detailed claim: within 42 days (1999) or as stated (2017)
- Engineer’s response / determination under Sub-Clause 3.5 / 3.7
- Variations instructed under Clause 13 generally not subject to the Clause 20 time-bar

## Typical Indian / CPWD / NHAI forms
- Notice periods often shorter (15–30 days)
- Many forms apply time limits to all claims including extras and deviations
- Always check the specific Contract Data / Special Conditions

## Key Indian law points
- Indian Contract Act s.55 (time as essence / prevention principle)
- Courts may relax strict time-bars if no prejudice shown
- Contemporaneous records are decisive in arbitration
