# Quantum Principles for Claims

- Direct costs: actual additional expenditure supported by invoices, timesheets, plant records.
- Prolongation costs: only for periods of Employer Risk delay that are not concurrent.
- Formulae (Hudson, Emden, Eichleay) accepted by Indian courts only when actual loss of opportunity or unabsorbed overhead is proved by contemporaneous evidence (McDermott, Unibros, Batliboi).
- Disruption: measured-mile or productivity analysis preferred over global claims.
- Loss of profit: requires proof of alternative work that was available and declined because of the delay.
- Never award quantum on assertion alone.
