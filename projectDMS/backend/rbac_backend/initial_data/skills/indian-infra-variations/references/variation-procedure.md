# Variation Procedure Notes

## FIDIC
- Clause 13.1 – Right to Vary
- Clause 13.3 – Variation Procedure (proposal, instruction, valuation)
- Engineer’s determination under 3.7
- Value Engineering under 13.2

## Indian forms (CPWD / NHAI EPC)
- “Change of Scope” or “Deviation” clauses
- Often require prior approval of competent authority for large variations
- Rate analysis for extra items commonly follows departmental schedules or market rates with overheads and profit

## Practical tips
- Convert constructive variations into formal instructions promptly.
- In lump-sum/EPC contracts the scope is defined by drawings and Employer’s Requirements, not BOQ quantities.
- Keep a variation register with status, valuation basis and time impact.
