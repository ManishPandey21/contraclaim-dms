# Standard Pleading Structure Notes

## Statement of Claim
- Keep each claim self-contained.
- Always plead notice compliance expressly.
- Quantify every head of claim (INR and/or days).
- Attach a detailed chronology as an annexure if the main body becomes lengthy.

## Statement of Defence
- Raise all preliminary objections at the outset.
- Use clear admissions and denials; avoid vague language.
- Plead the positive case (what actually happened) rather than only denying the Claimant’s version.
- If relying on concurrent delay or contractor-risk events, plead the facts and the contractual consequence.

## Rejoinder
- Limited to answering new points.
- Do not introduce entirely new claims unless permitted by the tribunal.

## Counterclaim
- Must be independently maintainable.
- Follow the same structure and evidential standards as a SOC.
