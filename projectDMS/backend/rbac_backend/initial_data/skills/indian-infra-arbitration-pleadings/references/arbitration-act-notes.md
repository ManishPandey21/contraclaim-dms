# Key Notes – Arbitration & Conciliation Act 1996 (India)

- Section 16: Kompetenz-kompetenz – tribunal can rule on its own jurisdiction.
- Section 23: Statements of claim and defence – parties may amend with leave.
- Section 28: Tribunal decides in accordance with the terms of the contract and applicable law (Indian Contract Act, specific contract terms).
- Time-bar / limitation: governed by the Limitation Act 1963; contractual time-bars are examined for prejudice and reasonableness.
- Interim measures: Section 17 (tribunal) and Section 9 (court).
- Award must be reasoned (Section 31).

Always ensure pleadings are consistent with the seat (usually India) and the institutional rules if any (ICA, DIAC, ad-hoc, etc.).
