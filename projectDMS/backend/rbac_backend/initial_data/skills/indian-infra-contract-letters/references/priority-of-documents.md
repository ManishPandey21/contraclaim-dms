# Priority of Documents – Quick Reference

## FIDIC 1999 / 2017 Red Book (typical)
1. Contract Agreement
2. Letter of Acceptance
3. Letter of Tender
4. Particular Conditions Part A – Contract Data
5. Particular Conditions Part B – Special Provisions
6. General Conditions
7. Specification
8. Drawings
9. Schedules / Bill of Quantities
10. Any other documents forming part of the Contract

## Typical Indian / CPWD / NHAI hierarchy
1. Agreement / Contract Agreement
2. Letter of Acceptance / Notice to Proceed
3. Contractor’s Bid / Letter of Tender
4. Contract Data / Particular Conditions
5. Special Conditions of Contract
6. General Conditions of Contract
7. Specifications
8. Drawings
9. Bill of Quantities
10. Any other documents listed in the Contract Data

Always check the actual contract’s Priority clause first. In case of ambiguity the Engineer issues clarification.
