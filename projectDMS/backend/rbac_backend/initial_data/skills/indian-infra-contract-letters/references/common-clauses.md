# Common Clauses for Letter Drafting

## FIDIC
- 1.3 – Notices and other Communications (form, delivery, timing)
- 1.5 – Priority of Documents
- 3.5 / 3.7 – Determinations
- 8.4 – Extension of Time for Completion
- 13 – Variations and Adjustments
- 20.2 – Claims for Payment and/or EOT (notice within 28 days)

## CPWD GCC (typical)
- Clause 5 – Time and Extension for Delay
- Clause 12 – Deviations / Variations
- Notice requirements under relevant clauses and SOP 5/25 for EOT applications

## NHAI / MoRTH EPC
- Clause on Commencement, Site Handover, Extension of Time, Change of Scope, Claims
- Strict notice periods usually apply; check the specific Agreement.

Always quote the exact sub-clause number and key wording in the letter.
