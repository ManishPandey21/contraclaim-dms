# Concurrent Delay – Indian Practice

True concurrent delay = two or more independent delay events (one Employer Risk Event, one Contractor Risk Event) whose delaying effects are felt on the critical path at the same time.

Dominant approach followed by Indian tribunals and courts (influenced by SCL Protocol):

- The Contractor remains entitled to an Extension of Time for the concurrent period.
- The Contractor is generally not entitled to prolongation costs, head-office overheads or loss of profit for the concurrent period unless it can isolate additional costs caused solely by the Employer Delay.
- The Employer cannot levy liquidated damages for the concurrent period attributable to its own delay.

Always prepare a clear concurrency matrix showing overlapping periods, critical-path impact, and net entitlement.

Prevention principle (Indian Contract Act s.55) reinforces that the Employer cannot benefit from its own delaying acts.
