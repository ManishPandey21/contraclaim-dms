# ContraClaim DMS

Contract correspondence and document management for EPC, infrastructure and claims teams, with
AI-assisted drafting. This glossary fixes the words used for who may act, and where.

## Language

### Platform roles

**Super Admin**:
The platform operator role with authority over every organisation and project at once. It is the
only role that performs global administration, and it is held by named platform operators only.
_Avoid_: Super User, superuser, global admin, system admin

**Super User**:
A multi-organisation role whose reach is bounded to the organisations explicitly granted to the
account. Dormant: defined but not supported, and no account may hold it.
_Avoid_: Super Admin, global administrator
